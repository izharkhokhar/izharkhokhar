## Current Projects

### [AI Lead-to-Appointment Automation](https://github.com/izharkhokhar/ai-lead-to-appointment-automation)
An end-to-end n8n automation that:

- Captures incoming business leads
- Uses AI to classify leads as HIGH, NORMAL or LOW priority
- Stores leads in Google Sheets CRM
- Sends personalised email responses
- Handles Calendly bookings and cancellations
- Sends appointment reminders
- Automates post-consultation follow-up

**Tech:** n8n, OpenAI, Google Sheets, Gmail, Calendly, Webhooks
